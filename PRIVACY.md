# Alocir Privacy Policy

_Effective 17 November 2026_

Alocir is a disk space visualizer for macOS, Windows and Linux. This policy
covers every version of Alocir, including the one distributed through the Mac
App Store.

## Summary

**Alocir does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.**

## What Alocir does with your files

- Alocir reads the names and sizes of files in the folders you choose to scan,
  so it can draw a map of what uses your disk space.
- This information stays in your computer's memory while the app is open. It is
  never sent anywhere and is discarded when you close the window.
- On the Mac (version 2.0 and later), Alocir remembers up to ten recently
  scanned folders so you can open them again: each folder's path, its total
  size, when it was scanned, and the macOS permission to read it again. This
  list is kept in Alocir's preferences on your Mac only, and you can clear it
  at any time with **Clear Recents** in Settings or **File → Open Recent →
  Clear Menu**. Your appearance and colour settings are kept there too.
- Alocir only changes files when you explicitly ask it to, through its
  right-click menu: **Rename** renames the item and **Move to Trash** moves it
  to your system's Trash, where you can restore it.

## Network access

Alocir has no network access. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking,
crash reporting or third-party SDKs, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

## Data shared with Apple

If you install Alocir from the Mac App Store or TestFlight and have chosen to
share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may provide
the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. This is
handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
and can be turned off in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics &
Improvements**.

## Children

Alocir collects no data from anyone, including children.
